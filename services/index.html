Browse water damage services by cause

Every water damage job begins with the same questions. The published ANSI/IICRC S500 standard frames them the same way. Where did the water come from, how far has it spread and what is it touching? The services below are grouped by cause, because the cause decides how the water is removed, how the structure is dried and what has to be replaced.

Safety first

What to do before you call

  • Stay out of water near outlets, appliances and the electrical panel.
  • Close the main valve or the shutoff for the fixture that is leaking.
  • Photograph the damage before you move anything.
  • Lift small items off wet floors and keep children and pets away.

Then call (732) 352-9660 and describe what you see. Do not use a household vacuum on standing water, and do not walk under a ceiling that bulges.
