What to have ready when you call

  • Your street address and nearest cross street.
  • The source of the water, if you know it.
  • How deep the water is and which floors it touched.
  • Whether the water is clean, dirty or smells like sewage.
  • Whether anything electrical is wet.
  • Your insurer's name, if you plan to file a claim.

Emergency or not?

If water is flowing, flooding a room or coming through a ceiling, treat it as an emergency and call right away. If you have an older stain, a musty smell or a question about mold, call during the day and we talk it through.

Safety first

If water is near outlets, a panel or appliances, stay out of the area. Call from a dry room. If you smell gas or see sparks, leave the building and call 911.
