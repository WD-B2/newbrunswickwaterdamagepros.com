Privacy Policy

How New Brunswick Water Damage Pros handles information from calls and from this website. Last updated November 13, 2026.

Who we are

This website is operated by New Brunswick Water Damage Pros, 303 George Street, New Brunswick, NJ 08901. You can reach us at (732) 352-9660 or contact@newbrunswickwaterdamagepros.com.

Information we collect

When you call our line, we collect the phone number you call from and the details you give us about your property and the problem. Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes.

When you visit this website, our hosting provider and standard web tools may collect technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device type, pages viewed and the time of your visit. We do not collect information through online forms, because this site does not have any.

How we use information

  • To answer your call and understand your water damage problem.
  • To connect you with the licensed and insured professionals who perform the work.
  • To improve the quality of our service and train our staff.
  • To keep this website secure and working properly.
  • To meet legal obligations.

Sharing information

We share information with the contractors and service providers who handle your request, and with vendors that help us run our phone line and website. We may disclose information if the law requires it or to protect rights and safety.

Cookies and analytics

This website may use basic cookies or similar technologies for performance and security. You can control cookies in your browser settings. Third-party services such as embedded maps and fonts may receive your IP address when the page loads.

Retention and security

We keep call records and related notes for as long as needed for the purposes above and for legal and business reasons. We use reasonable safeguards, but no system is completely secure.

Your choices

You may ask us what information we hold about you, and you may ask us to correct or delete it, subject to legal requirements. Contact us by phone or email. You may tell us at the start of a call if you do not want to be recorded.

Children

This website is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. See also our terms of service.
