A flooded basement is an emergency, because water rises, soaks stored items and reaches electrical panels and furnaces. Our line is open 24/7. Do not go down if water is near outlets or the panel, shut off the source if it is a plumbing leak, and call from upstairs.

Why basements flood in New Brunswick

The city sits on the south bank of the Raritan River, and low basements near the river or on low streets are exposed to groundwater and runoff. Heavy rain can raise the water table, overload drains and push water through floor cracks and walls.

Groundwater and hydrostatic pressure

Wet soil pushes water through the floor and wall joints.

Sump pump failure

A pump that stops during a storm lets the pit overflow.

Surface runoff

Poor grading and clogged gutters send water to the foundation.

Plumbing failures

Water heaters, washers and supply lines leak into finished basements.

Emergency basement flooding: what to do first

  1. Stay safe above the water. Do not enter if water covers outlets, the electrical panel or appliances.
  2. Find the source if it is plumbing. Close the main valve if the water is clean and still running.
  3. Move what you can from upstairs. Do not carry heavy items through water.
  4. Call us. We pump, extract and start drying. The line is open 24/7.

Cleanup order in a finished or unfinished basement

Pumps remove deep water first. Extractors then pull water out of carpet and low spots. Wet drywall is cut above the waterline, insulation and carpet pad are removed and the concrete slab, framing and remaining walls are dried with dehumidifiers and air movers.

What affects the cost of basement water damage cleanup

Cost depends on the depth of water, the finished area, the amount of material removed, whether the water is clean or contaminated and how long drying takes. We explain the scope and price before work starts. Waterproofing and foundation repair are separate.

Basement water after heavy rain near the Raritan

Hurricane Ida in 2021 sent the Raritan over its banks in New Brunswick, and parts of the city were evacuated. Basement flooding in such events is often a mix of river water, runoff and drain overflow.

Neighborhoods such as the Fifth Ward, Feaster Park, Raritan Gardens, Edgebrook and Westons Mills each have their own mix of house styles. What matters inside any of them is how quickly water is removed and how well the slab and walls are dried.

Protecting furnaces, panels and stored items in a flooded basement

Furnaces, water heaters and electrical panels often sit low in a basement. Water that reaches them can damage controls and wiring, and a licensed electrician or HVAC technician should check equipment that stood in water before it is used again. Do not restart them yourself.

Stored boxes, books and cardboard absorb water quickly and are hard to save. Photograph them for your records, then move salvageable items to a dry place. Plastic bins and solid furniture can usually be cleaned and dried.

Insurance note for basement water

Standard homeowners policies generally exclude flood and groundwater seepage, according to the New Jersey Department of Banking and Insurance. A sudden plumbing release is often treated differently. Read your policy and ask what applies.

ANSI/IICRC S500 treats water that rises from the ground as Category 3. That is why flooded basements call for removal of porous materials and careful sanitizing.
