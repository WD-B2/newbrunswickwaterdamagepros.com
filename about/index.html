About New Brunswick Water Damage Pros

We answer water damage calls for New Brunswick, NJ and nearby towns. This page explains how we work.

Call (732) 352-9660

What we do

New Brunswick Water Damage Pros answers calls from homeowners, tenants, landlords and businesses who have water where it should not be. We help with water extraction, structural drying, flood and sewage cleanup, burst pipe and appliance leaks, storm water and mold remediation.

Our call line is open 24/7. We listen to what happened, explain the next step and send licensed and insured restoration professionals to handle the work.

How we work

  1. Scope and price first. We explain the scope of work and the price before work starts, so you know what you are agreeing to.
  2. Measure, do not guess. Moisture meters and thermal imaging show where water traveled. Drying ends when readings reach normal levels.
  3. Document the job. Photos and moisture logs help with insurance claims and later questions.
  4. Explain, then act. We tell you what must be removed, what can be dried and why.

Standards we point to

Two published standards guide professional work in this field. ANSI/IICRC S500 covers water damage restoration, and S520 covers mold remediation. Ask any company you consider whether its crews work to them. We encourage that question.

In New Jersey, contractors who do home improvement work must register with the Division of Consumer Affairs. Registration is not an endorsement, but it is a check you can make before hiring anyone.

What to do when you call, and how a typical call goes

You tell us the address, the source of the water and what is wet. We ask whether anything electrical is involved and whether the water looks clean or dirty. Then we explain the next step, which may be as simple as closing a valve and lifting items off the floor while a crew is arranged.

On site, the crew confirms the scope, explains the price and starts extraction. Drying equipment is set and readings are recorded. You get a clear picture of what is being removed and why before any demolition starts.

Properties we help

Houses and rowhouses

Basements, kitchens, bathrooms and ceilings after leaks, storms and floods.

Apartments and rentals

Leaks that reach neighboring units, with coordination between owners, tenants and managers.

Offices and shops

Water in occupied commercial space, with work planned around business hours.

Institutional and multi-unit buildings

Larger losses that need phased work and clear documentation.

What to check before hiring any restoration company

  • A written scope and price before work starts.
  • Whether the company follows the S500 and S520 standards.
  • Registration with the New Jersey Division of Consumer Affairs for home improvement work.
  • Proof of licensing and insurance where required.
  • How moisture readings and photos will be recorded and shared.

A company that answers these questions plainly is easier to work with. If one avoids them, keep looking.

What we do not do

  • We do not give legal or insurance advice. Your policy and your insurer decide coverage.
  • We do not make health claims about mold. Health questions belong with a physician.
  • We do not promise that water or mold problems will never return. We dry, remove and document.
  • We do not repair plumbing or roofs. We coordinate with the trades that do.

New Brunswick is a city of older homes, rentals, hospitals, university buildings and downtown offices on the south bank of the Raritan River. Hurricane Ida in 2021 showed how fast a river city can flood. We built this service around the local mix of problems.
